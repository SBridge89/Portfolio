TMDB Analysis

Power BI based data analysis of a TMDB dataset.

Power BI DAX Power Query Data Modelling

Project Overview

The aim of this project was to explore movie performance across ratings, popularity, genre and audience voting behaviour, using the top 10,000 highest-rated titles from a public TMDB dataset. The raw data was sourced as a flat Excel export from Kaggle, then cleaned and restructured into a fact and dimension model,including a bridge table to resolve the many-to-many relationship between films and genres, before being brought into Power BI. The final report combines DAX-based analysis, including a custom Movie Quality Index, with genre-level comparisons, outlier detection and time-based trend analysis.

Data Modelling

The model separates transactional movie data from descriptive attributes using a fact table (Movies - F: popularity, release date, rating, vote count) linked to dimension tables for movie details (Movies - D) and genre. Because a film can belong to more than one genre, genre couldn't be modelled as a simple one-to-many relationship — a film tagged "Drama, Comedy" would need to appear correctly whichever genre a report is sliced by. This was handled with a bridge table (MovieBridgeGenre), giving each film one row per genre it belongs to, which resolves the many-to-many relationship between films and genres while keeping the fact table at a clean one-row-per-film grain.

Star Schema

  • Movies - F – movie transactions at film grain, holding popularity, release date, rating and vote count
  • Movies - D – descriptive film attributes, including title, overview and original language
  • Genre – the distinct list of genres used for filtering and slicing
  • MovieBridgeGenre – resolves the many-to-many relationship between films and genres, since a film can carry more than one genre
  • Date – calendar and time intelligence, used for year-over-year and rolling-average measures

Summary

Provides an overview of the TMDB dataset, highlighting key movie, rating, popularity, voting and genre metrics. This page establishes the overall scale and characteristics of the dataset before exploring the more detailed analysis.

Genre and Language

Explores the distribution of movies across genres and languages, identifying the most represented categories and examining how the dataset varies across different movie markets.

Vote Share

Analyses how audience voting is distributed across the dataset, highlighting the movies and genres that account for the largest share of total votes and providing insight into audience engagement.

Genre Analysis

Examines movie performance across genres, comparing ratings, popularity, movie volumes and audience engagement to identify differences in how genres perform within the dataset.

Movie Performance Analysis

Evaluates movie performance using rating, popularity and voting metrics. Includes a comparison against a qualified rating benchmark, a Movie Quality Index combining rating and popularity, and analysis of how movie popularity is concentrated across different levels of audience voting.

Year Over Year Analysis

Analyses changes in movie releases and audience ratings over time, using year-over-year measures to identify changes in production volume and average movie performance.

Rolling Average and Rank Within Genre

Uses a three-year rolling average to examine longer-term changes in movie ratings while ranking movies within their respective genres. This provides both a broader view of rating trends and a more focused comparison of individual movie performance.

Movie Rating Outlier Analysis

Identifies movies whose rating and popularity differ substantially from the overall dataset, highlighting high-popularity/low-rating and low-popularity/high-rating outliers for further investigation.

Genre Contribution to Rating Distribution

Explores how individual genres contribute to the overall distribution of movie ratings, considering both their share of the movie population and their contribution to audience voting.

Time-Based Cohort Comparison

Groups movies into release-period cohorts to compare how different generations of movies perform across rating, popularity, voting and movie volume metrics.

Hit Ratio

Measures the proportion of movies within each genre that achieve a defined rating threshold, providing a consistent way to compare the frequency of higher-rated movies across genres.

DAX

DAX measures were used to create the majority of measures ranging from basic aggregation functions to time based functions to do year on year calculations. Some complex measures used variables, aggregator functions such as SUMX and filter modifiers such as ALL and REMOVEFILTERs.
